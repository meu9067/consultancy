Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026.

This is a static, single-page brochure site. It does not use cookies, does not run third-party analytics or advertising scripts, and does not collect personal data through any form on this site — there isn't one. This policy explains the small amount of data that is processed and why.

What data is processed

  • Theme preference. If you use the dark/light mode toggle, your choice is saved in your browser's localStorage so it's remembered on your next visit. This never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.
  • Standard server logs. Like virtually all web hosting, the server/CDN serving this site may log basic technical request data (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) for security and operational purposes. These logs are not used for tracking or profiling.
  • Google Fonts. This site loads the "Atkinson Hyperlegible" typeface from Google Fonts' fonts.googleapis.com/fonts.gstatic.com. Loading a font from Google's servers means your browser makes a request to Google, which may process the associated technical request data (such as your IP address) under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Contact details you choose to send. If you email or message via LinkedIn/GitHub using the links on this site, whatever you send is processed the same way any email or direct message is — it isn't collected by this website itself.

Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA, you have rights under the GDPR (access, correction, deletion, objection) over any personal data processed in connection with this site. Given how little is processed, this mostly concerns the server log and Google Fonts requests described above. To ask a question or exercise a right, contact {{name}} at {{email}}.

Changes to this policy

If what this site collects or how it's hosted changes, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
